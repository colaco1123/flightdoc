#1criticalCould not take off level at 0:41.3

13 s after throttle-up the controller was driving motor 1 to 63% with motor 3 at idle and the craft still would not lift that side. On a fresh build or after a rebuild this is nearly always motor order, motor direction or props on the wrong motors.

Evidence

  • the gyro barely moved, so the craft was resting on something rather than flying
  • 6 such episodes, 38 s in total
What to check
  • Check motor order against the mixer diagram in the configurator (motor tab, spin each motor individually)
  • Check each motor spins the right way (props-in or props-out as configured)
  • Check each prop is the right hand for its motor
  • Check the flight controller orientation (board alignment) matches how it is mounted

What to check

  • Compare motor 1 with the others: a weak motor, bent shaft or damaged prop makes the controller push it harder
  • If diagonal pairs differ (both CW motors high), the motors are twisted on the arms and the copter is fighting a yaw imbalance
