#1warningCompass interference from motors/power wiring

The magnetic field reading moves 6% with current: the compass is picking up current in nearby wires. This causes toilet-bowling and fly-aways in GPS modes.

Evidence
  • magnetometer reading shifts by 6% of the earth field across the current range (current explains 1% of the variation)
  • autopilot switched between compasses 84 times
  • 2 EKF yaw resets
  • the estimator rejected mag data as a result
What to check
  • Move the compass (usually in the GPS puck) further from the battery leads, ESCs and power distribution
  • Twist battery and motor power wires together
  • Run compass/motor calibration (COMPASS_MOT on ArduPilot) and keep interference under 30%

Read more: ArduPilot: compass/motor calibration (compassmot)
