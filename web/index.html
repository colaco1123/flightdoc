Connect a flight controller over USB

Plug the board in, close Betaflight / INAV Configurator or Mission Planner (they hold the port), then connect. Works in Chrome and Edge.

Download report

Your log stays on your computer: Python runs inside the browser through WebAssembly (Pyodide), so the first run downloads about 30 MB of libraries, then it is cached. Diagnosis is automated; confirm it on the craft before you fly again.

Open source on GitHub. Got a crash log with a known cause that flightdoc gets wrong? Open an issue.
